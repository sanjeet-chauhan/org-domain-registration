## What Is a .org Domain?

.org is one of the oldest domain extensions on the internet. It launched in 1985 and is managed by the Public Interest Registry (PIR).

It was built for non-profits, but anyone anywhere can register one. You do not have to be a registered charity.

| Detail | .org |
| --- | --- |
| Managed by | Public Interest Registry (PIR) |
| Launched | 1985 |
| Who can register | Anyone worldwide |
| Best for | NGOs, charities, foundations, schools, religious groups, open source projects, community groups |
| Activation | Instant after purchase |

Visitors read .org as "this site exists to serve a purpose." For cause-driven organizations, that first impression lowers donor hesitation before anyone reads a word.

## How to Transfer Your .org Domain to YouStable

You can move a .org domain you already own, and your website stays live throughout.

1. **Unlock the domain** with your current registrar.
2. **Request the authorization code** (also called the EPP code).
3. **Start the transfer** from your YouStable account and enter the code.
4. **Approve it** when you receive the confirmation, and wait for the transfer to complete.

Keep your contact email current during the transfer, because confirmation messages go there.

## Renewal: What Happens After Year One

Standard renewal charges apply from year two. To avoid losing your name, plan ahead.

- **Turn on auto-renew,** so the domain renews before it expires.
- **Watch for reminders.** YouStable sends advance renewal notices.
- **Do not let it lapse.** An expired .org domain enters a grace period, then a redemption period, and is then released to the public.

For a non-profit, losing your domain can mean losing donor links, email addresses and years of reputation. Renewing on time is cheap insurance.

## A Note on SEO

A .org domain does not give you a ranking boost by itself, and search engines treat it as a normal domain extension. What it can help with is trust, because people are more likely to click a site that looks mission-driven.

Rankings still depend on your content, your links and your website speed. Pair your domain with reliable hosting for the full benefit.
